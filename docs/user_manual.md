# 📘 PDF Bookmark Manager - Comprehensive User Manual

Welcome to the PDF Bookmark Manager! This program is your personal assistant for reading PDF documents, allowing you to highlight important sections, insert bookmarks, take notes, and even extract text from scanned images, just as if you were working with a real physical book.

If you are opening the program for the first time, please follow this step-by-step guide.

## 🟢 SECTION 1: Getting to Know the Program and First Steps

When you open the program, you will be greeted by the **"PDF Study Library"** screen. This is your virtual desk.

### 1. Language Selection

If the program's language is not suitable for you, you can instantly change it by clicking the dropdown menu (🇹🇷 TR / 🇬🇧 EN) in the top right corner of the screen.

### 2. Opening Your First PDF Document

To start working, we need to put a book on the desk:

1. Click the **`Open PDF`** button in the top left corner of the screen.
2. Select any PDF file you want to read from your computer and click "Open".
3. Your document will open in a large view on the right side of the screen.

## 🟢 SECTION 2: Reading and Navigating Pages

Now that your document is open, how do we turn the pages?

* **Changing Pages:** You can use the **`← Previous`** and **`Next →`** buttons on the top menu. Alternatively, you can type the exact page number you want to go to in the middle box and press `Enter` on your keyboard.

* **Zoom In / Zoom Out:** Is the text too small? Click the **`+ Zoom In`** or **`- Zoom Out`** buttons at the top. *(Shortcut: You can hold down the `Ctrl` key on your keyboard while scrolling your mouse wheel up or down).*

* **Panning the Page (Hand Tool):** To scroll the page up, down, left, or right, click and hold your **right mouse button** and drag. The page will slide smoothly, just like pushing a piece of paper with your hand.

## 🟢 SECTION 3: Organizing Your Work (Groups)

Before saving important spots, we should create folders (groups) for ourselves. This keeps everything tidy and organized.

1. Click the **`Add Group`** button on the top menu.
2. Type a name in the window that appears (e.g., "Important Contracts" or "Visa Notes") and click OK.
3. You will see your new group appear in the wide empty space on the left side of the screen (the Bookmarks Panel).

* *Pro Tip:* You can right-click on the group you created and select "New Subgroup" to create folders within folders.

## 🟢 SECTION 4: Adding Bookmarks and Taking Notes

While reading the PDF, you come across a very important sentence and want to be able to find it again later. Here is what you need to do:

### How to Add a Bookmark?

1. From the list on the left side of the screen, click once on the **group** where you want to save this bookmark (the group will turn blue).
2. Now, on the PDF on the right side, left-click and drag your mouse to **select the important text** (it will be covered in a blue highlight).
3. **Right-click** on this blue selected area.
4. Click the **`Create Bookmark from Selection`** option from the menu that appears.

* *Congratulations! The sentence you selected has just been added as a record to your list on the left.*

### Taking Notes on a Bookmark

1. Click once on your newly created bookmark on the left side.
2. You will notice that the **"📝 Bookmark Notes"** box in the bottom left corner becomes active.
3. **Double-click** inside this white box.
4. You can now type your personal notes and summaries here using your keyboard.
5. When you are finished, don't forget to click the **`💾 Save Note`** button right below it.

## 🟢 SECTION 5: Advanced Features (Copying, Highlighting, Excel)

The most powerful features of the program are revealed when you select text on the PDF and **right-click**. Select an area you are reading with your mouse and right-click:

### 1. Standard Copy (`Copy`)

If you are reading a normal, digitally created PDF, use this to copy the selected text. When you paste the text into Word or LibreOffice, its original colors and bold formatting will be transferred perfectly without breaking.

### 2. Copying by Reading from an Image (`📷 Copy with OCR`)

**Very Important:** Sometimes you receive a scanned document, a photograph, or a PDF with locked text copying. You might not be able to select the text, or when you copy it, meaningless characters appear.

* In such a case, select the area, right-click, and choose the **`📷 Copy with OCR (From Image)`** option. The program will read the text from the image using its optical engine and convert it into clean text for you.

### 3. Highlighter (`Highlight Selection`)

Click this button to permanently highlight the selected area with a yellow marker. If you make a mistake, you can right-click on the yellow area again and select "Remove Highlight".

### 4. Exporting Tables to Excel (`Export Selected Table to Excel`)

Do you see an invoice table or a data list in the PDF? Select the entire table with your mouse, right-click, and press this button. The program will automatically clean all the numbers and texts in the table and provide you with a ready-to-use Excel (`.xlsx`) file.

## 🟢 SECTION 6: Searching and Exporting

### Searching

* **Searching within the PDF:** Type a word into the search box in the bottom right corner of the screen and press `Enter` (or the Find button). The program will find all occurrences of the word in the PDF and list them page by page. Double-clicking a result in the list will take you directly to that page.

* **Searching within Bookmarks:** By typing in the search box in the top left, you can search through hundreds of notes and bookmarks you have created in seconds.

### Exporting Your Work (Generating a Report)

You have worked for days, taken notes, and collected bookmarks. Do you want to send them to someone else or view them as a summary?

1. Click the **`📤 Export (.md)`** button on the top menu.
2. The program neatly combines all the notes, groups, and page numbers you created for that PDF and presents them as a professional study report file.

### Printing

By clicking the **`🖨 Print`** button on the top menu, you can send the current page you are reading, a specific page range (e.g., 5-15), or the entire document to your printer.
